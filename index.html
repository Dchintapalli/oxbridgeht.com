Oxbridge HT / In development
02 / What we're building

Equity, by design.

At Oxbridge HT, we're developing an equitable pulse-oximetry concept that explores how optical sensing and device design can better account for variation in skin tone.

The project reflects our wider approach: begin with a meaningful care problem, examine the engineering and evidence challenges, and build with real-world use in mind.

03 / About Oxbridge HT

Built at the intersection of care and engineering.

Oxbridge HT is led by a founder with a background in electronics and communication engineering, an MBA and Healthcare Innovation study at the University of Cambridge.

The founder's experience spans NHS England, healthtech accelerator delivery and hands-on work developing an equitable pulse-oximetry concept. That mix of systems thinking and technical curiosity shapes how we approach new challenges.
